01Two kinds

Two products that look alike and are not

The most common failure in this field is arguing about TouchDesigner versus Unreal, or about pose-estimation accuracy, before deciding which of the two you are making.

① Experience

Something people experience

The exhibit you see in a museum or at an event, where video and sound react to how people move. Projection mapping, installations, stage visuals, experience-driven apps.

The goal is to make a visitor feel something.

② Training tool

A tool that makes people better

For things where form matters — dance, martial arts, sports, yoga. It films you, evaluates what you did, tells you what to fix and designs your practice.

The goal is that the person actually improves.

Example

“Make a dance app” is two different products

A tool for memorising choreography, or a piece people watch for pleasure? The skill will not end on “it could be either”. It picks one and tells you why — not picking is what costs the most.

If you already know what you are building, the director never appears. “Design an MVP for a form-comparison app for a dojo” starts the training-tool skill directly.

02Architecture

One director, two specialists

The two specialists do the design work. The director on top only decides which way you go. They are deliberately not split further: every extra description in the system prompt makes the hardest call — experience or improvement — worse.

  • 00

    embodied-product-director

    Decides which of the two you are making, with the reason. Appears only when you have not decided yet.

  • ①

    interactive-experience-collective

    Exhibits, installations, stage visuals and experience apps: projection, latency, audio, venue economics.

  • ②

    movement-learning-system-designer

    Form evaluation, practice design and tools that help instructors — who uses it, who pays, what is actually going wrong today.

Skill instructions are in English; the reference library is still in Japanese (translation in progress). Claude reads both and answers in your language. Japanese trigger phrases are kept, so Japanese prompts start the skills too.

03Install

Two lines, then just describe it

The plugin installs all three skills. Open a new session and write what you are building normally — the right skill starts on its own.

claude code
/plugin marketplace add takaoumehara/interactive-experience-skills
/plugin install interactive-experience-skills@interactive-experience

Recommended. Installs the three skills as one plugin.

bash
git clone https://github.com/takaoumehara/interactive-experience-skills.git
cd interactive-experience-skills
./install.sh

Also installs the maintainer commands /motion-idea, /refresh-skills, /scout-skills and /skills-routine. Don't install both the plugin and the script copies.

bash
./package.sh   # writes dist/<skill>.zip

Upload each zip in your assistant's skill settings.

04What you get

A design decision, not code

What comes back is what to build, roughly what equipment and how much of it, what to validate first, and what not to build yet. Implementation is a normal Claude Code job afterwards.

Commit

It always commits to one

Never “well, it could be either.” It picks experience or training tool, and gives the reason.

Numbers

Numbers, not “immersive”

5,000–8,000 lm for 3 m in a dark room. Feedback within 100 ms. A side camera for step depth. About 107,000 characters across 18 references, loaded only when needed.

Refuse

It says no clearly

No judging pain or injury. Silence when unsure. Guardian consent before technology for children. And no belief that better pose accuracy means better learning.

05FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it write code?
No. It returns design decisions: what to build, what equipment, what to validate first and what not to build yet. Ask Claude Code to implement afterwards.
Do I have to call the director myself?
No. Describe what you are building and the right skill starts. The director only appears when you have not decided between an experience and a training tool.
Can I use it in Japanese?
Yes. Each skill keeps its Japanese trigger phrases, and the reference library is written in Japanese. Claude answers in the language you use.
Won't the prices and hardware numbers go stale?
Yes, so volatile passages are marked. When the skill reads one, it checks the web before answering. /refresh-skills batch-verifies them and /scout-skills proposes new candidates without editing references.
Will it assess injuries or rehabilitation?
No. Pain, injury and range of motion are medicine. It will not step into rehabilitation without a clinician involved.
How accurate is the routing between the three skills?
An earlier check was reported as 97% with 11% ambiguous cases, but its method, model, date and raw results were never committed, so treat it as to be re-measured. Each skill ships evals/evals.json with near-miss cases for re-checking.
